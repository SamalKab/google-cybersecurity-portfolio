# Course 1: Foundations of Cybersecurity

## Cybersecurity

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting systems, networks, and data from digital attacks.

## CIA Triad

The CIA Triad is a foundational security model used to protect information.

### Confidentiality
Ensures that information is accessible only to authorized users.

### Integrity
Ensures that data remains accurate, consistent, and trustworthy.

### Availability
Ensures that systems and data are accessible when needed.

---

## Security Frameworks

Security frameworks are guidelines used to help organizations manage risks and protect data and privacy.

Examples:
- NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF)
- Security controls
- Risk management processes

---

## Security Posture

Security posture describes an organization's ability to protect critical assets and manage cybersecurity risks.

# Course 2: Play It Safe: Manage Security Risks

## Risk Management

Risk management is the process of identifying, assessing, and reducing cybersecurity risks.

## Security Controls

Security controls are safeguards designed to reduce risks and protect systems and data.

## Playbooks

Playbooks are documented procedures that help security teams respond to specific incidents.

## Incident Response

Incident response is the process of preparing for, detecting, responding to, and recovering from security incidents.
